## QUIZ — "Are You Flying Blind?"

Format: 7 questions, ~60 seconds, one screen each, dark UI with gold progress bar. Result: **Titan Fit Score (0–100)** + one personalized blind spot.

**Q1. What runs your business today?** *(multi-select)*
Jobber · Housecall Pro · ServiceTitan · QuickBooks · Toast/Square/NCR · Spreadsheet · Pen and paper · Other
*(scoring: any selection = fit signal; "spreadsheet/paper" routes result copy toward Titan Native)*

**Q2. Do you know why revenue changed last week?**
Yes (0) · Somewhat (8) · No (15)

**Q3. Can you see profit per job/order?**
Yes (0) · No (15)

**Q4. Are calls, payments, documents, and customer history connected?**
Yes (0) · No (15)

**Q5. Do you know what your business is worth today?**
Yes (0) · No (15)

**Q6. Do employees get AI help doing the work?**
Yes (0) · No (10)

**Q7. Would you rather…**
Connect your current system · Run on Titan Native · See both
*(routing only — sets which CTA the results page leads with)*

**Scoring → Titan Fit Score** = 30 (base, any business with systems) + sum of Q2–Q6 points. Max 100.

**Result bands:**
- **75–100 — "Flying blind at full speed."** *"Your business generates answers every day — and almost none of them reach you. You're exactly who we built Titan for."*
- **45–74 — "Instruments, no autopilot."** *"You see what happened. The why — and the what-next — still lives in your head. Titan takes that weight."*
- **0–44 — "Sharper than most."** *"You run tighter than 90% of owners. Titan's job for you is compounding: memory, valuation, and the risks you haven't hit yet."*

**Results-page CTA:** `Book a 15-minute intelligence audit` + the Q7-routed secondary (`Connect your business` / `Run on Titan Native`).
