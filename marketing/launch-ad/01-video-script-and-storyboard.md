## SCENE 1 — CHAOS (0:00–0:08)

**Picture.** 6:30 AM. A locksmith owner's home office, lit only by monitor glow and a cold blue dawn through blinds. Handheld, shallow DOF, slight breathing on the lens. We see over his shoulder: 17 browser tabs (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, QuickBooks, Gmail), a phone screen stacking missed calls (4), technician texts ("collected cash on Elm St"), a sticky note that says "FOLLOW UP QUOTES??". His coffee is untouched. Close-up: his eyes scanning, reflected tabs sliding across his glasses.

**VO (male, low, unhurried — documentary intimacy):**
> "Your business is not broken. It's scattered."

**On-screen text (bottom-left, small mono type, terminal-style):**
`17 systems. 0 answers.`

**Sound.** Overlapping phone buzzes, mouse clicks, a muffled voicemail ("...hi, I'm locked out, call me back—"), low room tone. No music yet.

**Duration/shots.** 3 shots: (a) wide office 3s, (b) OTS screen chaos 3s, (c) eyes CU 2s.

## SCENE 2 — THE PROBLEM (0:08–0:15)

**Picture.** Stylized macro world: data physically *trapped*. Invoices frozen inside glass cubes. A calendar page under water. Call waveforms locked behind wire mesh. Receipts fluttering in a sealed jar. Each element floats in black void, lit with a single hard key light. Camera glides between them (dolly, 24mm).

**VO:**
> "You know what happened. But you don't know why."

**On-screen text (three lines, appearing one per second, white → gold on the third):**
`What changed?`
`Why did it change?`
`What should we do next?`

**Sound.** First music note: a single sub-bass pulse each time a text line lands. Glass resonance SFX as camera passes each trapped object.

## SCENE 3 — TITAN APPEARS (0:15–0:23)

**Picture.** Black frame. A hairline of Titan gold light draws a brain-mark logo. Then, from the edges of frame, luminous data-streams (each labeled with a tiny system wordmark: Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, QuickBooks, Toast, Square, Gmail, PDFs, Calls) arc inward like solar prominences and pour into the mark. The logo ignites.

**VO:**
> "Titan connects the systems you already use — and builds your business memory."

**On-screen text (rapid-fire, one per beat, then the alternative):**
`Bring Jobber. Bring ServiceTitan. Bring Housecall Pro. Bring QuickBooks. Bring Toast. Bring Square.`
`— or run on Titan Native.`

**Sound.** Music proper begins: dark analog synth pulse, 92 BPM. Rising shepard-tone under the data convergence. A deep, clean *thoom* when the logo ignites.

## SCENE 4 — BUSINESS MEMORY (0:23–0:31)

**Picture.** The signature shot. Inside a vast dark space, thousands of golden nodes ignite one by one — each a job, a call, an invoice, an email, a decision — connecting into a slowly rotating knowledge graph shaped faintly like a brain. Camera orbits, then pushes through the graph (particles part around the lens). Individual nodes flash micro-labels on approach: "Rekey — Mitchell — $175 — Paid", "Decision: raised smart-lock price", "10-K uploaded".

**VO:**
> "Every call. Every job. Every payment. Every document. Every decision. Remembered."

**On-screen text (center, serif-weighted, held 2s):**
`Business Memory™`

**Sound.** Choir-like pad enters under the synth. Soft chimes as nodes ignite (randomized, granular). The push-through gets a low whoosh.

## SCENE 5 — WHY BRAIN (0:31–0:40)

**Picture.** Cut to the real product (screen-capture style, re-lit in 3D glass). The owner types: *"Why was revenue down this week?"* Titan's tool timeline executes — `getRevenueSummary ✓ getJobsSummary ✓ getTechnicianPerformance ✓` — then a factor waterfall builds bar by bar: missed calls, technician delays, unpaid jobs, lower average ticket, quote follow-up failure. The primary-cause line draws itself in gold.

**VO:**
> "Titan doesn't just show numbers. It explains why they changed."

**On-screen text (as UI callouts, not supers):**
`Revenue down 11%.`
`Primary cause: missed calls + lower average ticket.`
`Estimated lost revenue: $8,420.`

**Sound.** Music tightens to a ticking hi-hat. Each tool-check lands a soft mechanical *click*. The $8,420 reveal gets a low piano note.

**Transition.** Match-cut: the owner's cursor becomes a set of van keys turning an ignition.

## MUSIC DIRECTION (full spot)

- **0:00–0:08** — No music. Diegetic chaos only. The absence is the tension.
- **0:08–0:15** — Single sub-bass pulses, one per on-screen question. ~40Hz, felt not heard.
- **0:15–0:31** — Main theme enters: dark analog synth arpeggio at 92 BPM (think Hans Zimmer × Stripe brand films), building with a choir pad through Business Memory.
- **0:31–0:57** — Rhythm section: ticking hi-hats, four-on-floor kick under the locksmith/HVAC sequences. Kinetic but controlled.
- **0:57–1:03** — Half-time drop for Risk Radar. Space + sonar.
- **1:03–1:22** — Full theme returns with a major-leaning chord lift on "Plan generated," opening into warmth for CEO Packet.
- **1:22–1:30** — Strip to one sustained note. Hard silence on the final line. Logo hit. Out.

Reference tracks for the composer/library search: "Time" (Zimmer) restraint, F1 title-sequence percussion, Apple "Don't Blink" pacing.
