## FABLE 5 PROMPTS — live-action / narrative scenes

**F1 · Scene 1a (wide, 3s)**
> 6:30 AM home office of a small-business owner, lit only by two monitors and cold blue dawn through half-open blinds. A man in his 40s, work jacket over a t-shirt, sits motionless in front of screen chaos — 17 browser tabs visible, phone face-up buzzing with a missed-call stack. Untouched coffee steams beside a sticky note reading "FOLLOW UP QUOTES??". Handheld camera, slight breathing movement, shallow depth of field, documentary realism.

**F1 · Scene 1b (over-shoulder, 3s)**
> Over-the-shoulder shot of the same man: monitor fills frame with overlapping software tabs — field-service dashboards, accounting ledgers, an inbox with 214 unread. His phone lights with a technician text: "collected cash on Elm St". Focus racks from screen to the back of his head. Tension without melodrama.

**F1 · Scene 1c (eyes CU, 2s)**
> Extreme close-up of tired eyes scanning left to right, browser-tab reflections sliding across his glasses in cold blue, a single amber notification reflection crossing at the end. Macro lens, cinematic.

**F5 · Scene 5 owner (2s insert)**
> The same owner, now half-lit in gold from the screen, leans in and types a question into a dark command bar. His face shifts from fatigue to focus. Close on hands, then eyes.

**F6 · Scene 6 sequence (5 quick shots, ~1.5s each)**
> (a) A dark suburban kitchen at night; a phone on the counter answers itself — an elegant gold waveform pulses on screen as a calm AI voice takes an emergency lockout call. (b) A locksmith van door slams; boots hit pavement. (c) A technician's phone in-hand showing a job checklist with gold checkmarks. (d) Golden-hour handoff of folded cash at a front door, shallow focus. (e) A smartwatch buzzing on the owner's wrist: "Cash verification required — $240". Kinetic pacing, real-world grit, F1-style cutting.

**F7 · Scene 7 sequence (5 quick shots, ~1.5s each)**
> (a) Heat shimmer over a rooftop HVAC condenser under harsh Texas sun. (b) A technician kneels at the unit, phone propped showing a diagnostic checklist. (c) Macro: multimeter probes touch a cylindrical capacitor, digits settle. (d) Phone camera photographs a model/serial nameplate; the photo snaps into a UI record. (e) The technician shows a tablet to a homeowner in a doorway — a maintenance-plan card visible. Documentary realism with commercial polish.

**F12 · Scene 12 (2 shots, 4s + 4s)**
> (a) The Scene-1 owner, same desk, next morning: one clean screen glowing gold-on-dark, everything else closed. He takes the first sip of his coffee, shoulders drop half an inch. Warm dawn now, not cold. Slow push-in. (b) The screen fills frame, UI recedes into black leaving a thin gold brain-mark logo. Stillness.

## HIGGSFIELD PROMPTS — 3D / UI / abstract product shots

**H2 · Trapped data (Scene 2, 7s)**
> Surreal macro world in a black void: paper invoices frozen inside crystal-clear glass cubes; a paper calendar page suspended underwater; audio waveforms imprisoned behind fine wire mesh; receipts fluttering inside a sealed bell jar. Single hard key light per object, dust motes, 24mm dolly glide between objects. Photoreal render, cinematic depth of field, charcoal-black environment, no text.

**H3 · Data convergence logo ignite (Scene 3, 8s)**
> A hairline of molten gold light draws a minimal brain-shaped logomark in a black void. From all frame edges, luminous ribbon-like data streams — each carrying faint ghosted labels (CRM cards, ledger rows, email threads, call waveforms, PDF pages) — arc inward like solar prominences and pour into the mark until it ignites with volumetric bloom. Slow-motion particle physics, lens flare restrained, 3D render, premium title-sequence energy.

**H4 · Business Memory graph (Scene 4, 8s) — SIGNATURE SHOT**
> Vast dark space. Thousands of small golden nodes ignite one by one and connect with fine light filaments, forming a slowly rotating knowledge-graph constellation faintly shaped like a human brain. Camera orbits 90°, then pushes directly through the graph as particles part around the lens. A few nodes flash tiny holographic labels on approach (a job ticket, a phone call, a signed document). Deep volumetric fog, gold-on-black, awe-scale, photoreal 3D.

**H5 · Why Brain UI flythrough (Scene 5, 7s)**
> Cinematic 3D flythrough of a dark glass dashboard floating in space: a command bar receives the typed question "Why was revenue down this week?"; a vertical tool-execution timeline ticks green-gold checkmarks (getRevenueSummary, getJobsSummary, getTechnicianPerformance); then a factor-waterfall chart builds bar by bar in gold and red. Callout chips: "Revenue down 11%", "Estimated lost revenue: $8,420". Glass-morphism panels, depth-of-field shifts between layers, no cursor, elegant motion design.

**H8 · Risk Radar (Scene 8, 6s)**

Blips pulse from red to gold when acknowledged. Subtle scanline texture, volumetric glow, premium HUD design, 3D parallax.

**H9 · Goal path (Scene 9, 7s)**
> Dark glass interface: a single input field receives "Sell for $5M." A large counter rolls up to "$1.6M current valuation," then a luminous gold path draws itself up-and-right across a dark topographic landscape with five glowing waypoint markers, each unfurling a small label card. Cinematic camera crane following the path. Motion-design precision, no clutter.

**H10 · Universal Cart assembly (Scene 10, 5s)**
> Product-photography void, charcoal backdrop: a translucent glass shopping cart assembles itself as photoreal items fly in and snap into place in rhythm — brass key blanks, a smart lock, HVAC filters, cylindrical capacitors, hand tools — each trailed by a small gold reason-tag. Studio lighting, macro detail, satisfying magnetic snaps, 3D render.

**H11 · CEO Packet composition (Scene 11, 7s)**
> A premium executive report composes itself on a dark desk surface viewed top-down: heavy paper-textured cards slide into a grid — Revenue, Profit, Cash Risk, Technician Ranking, Valuation, Next Actions — with embossed gold section numerals and fine typography. A timeline scrubber rewinds a day of activity above it. Tactile, print-quality realism, soft top light.

**H-LOGO · End card (Scene 12b, 4s)**
